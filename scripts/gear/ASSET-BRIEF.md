# Camera loadout asset requirements

The existing interactive selector works, but its custom camera geometry has not met
the requested photorealistic standard. These requirements describe replacement
assets; they do not authorize a purchase or commission.

## Equipment

- Owned bodies: Canon EOS R7 and EOS 40D, modeled independently from their actual
  front, rear, top, and side contours. Do not relabel another EOS model.
- Owned lenses: Canon EF 28–135mm f/3.5–5.6 IS USM and EF 70–200mm f/4L.
  The current site lists the latter as IS USM; confirm the exact generation against
  the owner's equipment before commissioning its markings and control layout.
- Additional lenses already listed on the site: Tamron SP 35mm f/1.4 Di USD and
  Canon EF 50mm f/1.8 II.
- Available equipment: Canon EOS C200 with an explicitly identified example
  EF 70–200mm f/2.8 variant. This equipment must remain in “What I can use.”
- Separate EF-to-RF adapter for the R7. Native EF attachment on the 40D and C200.

## Geometry and appearance

Supply separate bodies, lenses, and adapter, with a documented mounting plane and
real-world dimensions. Keep optical axes consistent and retain separate parts in
the editable source. The viewer must support freely rotating every configuration.

Match model-specific shell curvature, grip shape, screen proportions, viewfinder
recess, rear controls, doors, seams, strap mounts, and top controls. Lens switches
must sit in their actual molded recesses. Zoom and focus grips must reproduce the
appropriate pattern, position, and profile for each lens.

Provide PBR base color, roughness, normal, and applicable metalness/occlusion maps.
Keep printed markings sharp at close inspection. Painted and rubber surfaces must
not use exposed-metal response. Optical elements need plausible shallow curvature,
coatings, internal housing, and an appropriate diaphragm, without exaggerated
tunnel reflections. Do not bake directional studio lighting into the base color.

Deliver editable source plus textured GLB/glTF. Record triangle counts, texture
sizes, physical dimensions, author, provenance, and the exact license. Preview
renders alone cannot establish the quality of the downloadable mesh.

## Publication rights

The destination is a public portfolio and public source repository. Its browser
downloads model assets. Obtain terms that explicitly accommodate this distribution
before placing any purchased source or exported geometry into the repository.
Do not assume that a generic royalty-free purchase grants that permission.

## Acceptance

Compare each body and lens with real product photographs from front, rear, side,
and three-quarter views, at a fixed scale. Review the actual GLB in the website,
including close-up markings, lens swaps, body swaps, and both ownership tabs.

Reject wrong body silhouettes, floating panels or rings, intersecting components,
generic substituted controls, unreadable markings, incorrect lens variants, or
materials that only look convincing under a single favorable light or angle.
Passing a production build is necessary for integration, but does not establish
photorealism or reference accuracy.
